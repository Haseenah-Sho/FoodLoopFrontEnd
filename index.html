How FoodLoop Works

Three simple steps to rescue food and reduce waste

Food Providers Post

Restaurants and households, as food providers, list their surplus food - free or at a discounted price - before it goes to waste.

Customers Browse & Buy

Anyone can browse food items freely without an account. Sign in to claim free food or buy discounted meals - first come, first served.

Pick Up or Get Delivered

Collect your food at pickup or have it delivered. Food is saved, money is saved, waste is reduced.
