In short

  • Food on FoodLoop is posted by independent food providers, not by FoodLoop.

1. What FoodLoop is

FoodLoop is an online platform that lets restaurants and households ("food providers") offer surplus food, free or at a reduced price, to people who want it ("customers"). FoodLoop provides the platform that brings them together. FoodLoop is not a food provider, a restaurant, a seller of food, or a delivery company.

2. Food is provided by independent food providers

Every food item on FoodLoop is posted by a food provider. The food provider prepares, stores, describes, prices, and hands over or delivers the food. FoodLoop does not own, prepare, inspect, store, transport or handle any of it.

3. No guarantee about the food

FoodLoop does not promise that any food item is safe, fresh, of good quality, suitable for you, or accurately described. This includes ingredients, allergen information, storage instructions, "best before" times, portion sizes and photos. These details are entered by the food provider.

4. Issues between a customer and a food provider

When you order or claim food, the arrangement is between you and the food provider. FoodLoop is not a party to it and is not responsible for any problem that arises from it, including food that is unsafe, different from its description, late, missing, or not collected.

FoodLoop may, at its own discretion, look into a flagged order or remove a food provider or food item from the platform. Doing so does not make FoodLoop responsible for the food or for the outcome of any dispute.

5. What customers should do

  • Read the description, allergen information, storage instructions and "best before" time before you order.
  • If you have an allergy or special dietary need, ask the food provider directly. Do not rely on the listing alone.
  • Follow the storage and reheating instructions, and do not eat food you are unsure about.
  • Check the food when you collect or receive it, and report a problem promptly through the platform.

6. What food providers should do

  • Only post food that is safe to eat and within its stated "best before" time.
  • Describe the food honestly, including ingredients, allergens and how it should be stored.
  • Keep your listing, pickup times and delivery options accurate, and honour the orders you accept.
  • Follow any food safety and other rules that apply to you. You are solely responsible for the food you post.

7. Payments

Payments for discounted food are processed by a third-party payment provider, Paystack. Card details are entered on the payment provider's own page. FoodLoop does not control how the payment provider operates and is not responsible for its services.

Nothing in this disclaimer limits any right or responsibility that the law does not allow to be limited.
